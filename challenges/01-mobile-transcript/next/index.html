WhipScribe · UI Challenge 01

Mobile transcript reader — next

Five high-impact phone states that return space to reading while making key moments easier to find, quote, and resume.

Core job: Find a moment → Read → Quote → Resume

Improve the existing reader through restraint: preserve WhipScribe’s transcript model and visual language, remove duplicated and persistent controls, and make the core reading flow more deliberate.

WHAT CHANGED

  • Reduced file chrome; Download moves into More.
  • Search remains one tap away; preview and player are compact.
  • A faint edge and stronger timestamp locate the current line.

WHY

The transcript is the primary mobile job. Space is returned to reading, without hiding a frequent task such as search.

WHAT WE KEPT

Filename, metadata, Transcript / Summary / AI Chat, timestamp gutter, preview model, and player concept.

WHAT CHANGED

  • The native-feeling selection toolbar adds Copy with timestamp and Share.
  • Playback, copy, and sharing remain compact actions around a selected excerpt.

WHY

A transcript quote is more useful when it preserves its source moment: 0:07 + “Stop at the top of the hour.”

WHAT WE KEPT

Direct text selection, standard Copy, Play from here, and lightweight sharing—not social cards or an AI workflow.

WHAT CHANGED

  • The boundary gives a concise, contextual reason to continue.
  • Continue leads to a value-first handoff rather than an immediate form wall.
  • Back is available at each point in the flow.

WHY

The account request follows a real interruption. “Keep your place” explains the value before authentication choices appear.

WHAT WE KEPT

Free preview, account requirement to continue, Google and email paths, and the recording context.

Deliberate scope

What we did not redesign

Standalone Search, Download, and Reading Settings; Load failure; Wrong account; Processing; Four-speaker state; and Desktop. They remain part of the product, but this submission focuses on the five highest-impact mobile moments.
